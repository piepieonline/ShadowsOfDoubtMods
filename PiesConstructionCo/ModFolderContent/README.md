# Piepieonline's PiesConstructionCo

A set of custom buildings for Shadows of Doubt, increasing the variety in the city

## Buildings:

- Shopping Plaza: A single story plaza with 4 retail spaces. Increases the number of retail outlets in the city without increasing citizens or offices
- Park + Amenities: A verson of the park that has a public toilet. Used a lot by the homeless, instead of them using business'
